Key Equations

Back EMF: Eb = Kb × N

KVL (any mode): Va = Eb + Ia·Ra (steady-state, large La)

Duty (motoring): α = Va / Vs

Duty (regen): α₂ = 1 − Va / Vs

On-time: ton = α × T, ton2 = α₂ × T

Pmech (mot.): Eb × Ia

Pmech (regen): Eb × |Ia| (braking input)

Copper loss: P_Ra = Ia² × Ra

Dynamic (ODE): La·dIa/dt = Va − Km·ω − Ra·Ia

J·dω/dt = Km·Ia − TL − B·ω
